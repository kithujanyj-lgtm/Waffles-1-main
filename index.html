Time for history

Waffles have a long and interesting history. Waffles originated from the ancient Greek flat cakes called obelios, evolved into medieval European wafers in the 9th - 10th centuries and later developed into modern waffles through innovations in Belgium, the Netherlands and the United States. Early waffle irons appeared in medieval France and Belgium, and the word "waffle" entered English in 1725.
